# Setup Guide

## Requirements

* n8n
* Google Forms
* Google Sheets
* Google Calendar
* Gmail
* Telegram Bot
* OpenAI API Key

## Setup Process

### Step 1

Create a Google Form with:

* Full Name
* Email
* Phone Number
* Appointment Date
* Appointment Time
* Service Required
* Service Price

### Step 2

Connect the form to Google Sheets.

### Step 3

Connect Google Sheets Trigger in n8n.

### Step 4

Configure OpenAI for appointment summaries.

### Step 5

Connect Google Calendar.

### Step 6

Connect Telegram Bot.

### Step 7

Connect Gmail.

### Step 8

Test the complete workflow.

## Expected Result

Appointments are captured, processed, scheduled, tracked, and reported automatically.
